# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, abridged)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in bat2ps a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Welcoming, inclusive language; respect for differing viewpoints.
- Graceful, constructive criticism focused on the work.
- Responsibility for mistakes, apologies, and learning.
- No harassment, insulting/derogatory comments, or unwelcome attention.
- No publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
Maintainers will review, warn, or ban contributors as appropriate and keep
reports confidential. This code applies in all project spaces.

(Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
